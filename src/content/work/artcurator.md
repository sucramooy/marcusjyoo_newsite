## Overview

Curate and install rotating art exhibitions across campus spaces, working with faculty to align placement with community needs and provide recognition for emerging local artists.

## What I do

- **Exhibition layout and installation** — plan and install shows across multiple campus venues, accounting for wall condition, lighting, sightlines, and flow
- **Artist coordination** — communicate with featured artists on delivery, handling, and installation requirements
- **Faculty collaboration** — align art placement with departmental and community priorities
- **Campus impact** — contribute to the daily environment for 2,000+ students and faculty

## What I learned

How to work within physical constraints (wall space, lighting, traffic patterns) while preserving the intent of the artwork. Curation is a design problem, every placement decision trades off visibility against context, and the right answer depends on what the viewer is meant to take away from the show.

## Overview

Routesetting is a blend of spatial design, biomechanics, and communication. Each route is a puzzle, you design movement sequences that challenge climbers at a specific difficulty grade while remaining safe and fair.

## Skills demonstrated

- **Biomechanics** — understanding how body positioning, reach, and leverage affect difficulty
- **Spatial reasoning** — visualizing 3D movement on a 2D wall layout
- **Safety** — ensuring routes are settable and climbable without unnecessary risk
- **Communication** — explaining route beta and adjusting based on climber feedback

## What I learned

How to design for a diverse range of body types and skill levels, and why iteration matters in physical design.
